Paracetamol

Uses:
Pain relief and fever reduction.

Dosage:
Adults: 500mg to 1000mg every 4-6 hours.
Maximum 4000mg per day.

Side Effects:
Nausea, rash, liver damage in overdose.


Ibuprofen

Uses:
Pain, inflammation, fever.

Dosage:
Adults: 200mg to 400mg every 4-6 hours.

Side Effects:
Stomach upset, dizziness, ulcers.


Amoxicillin

Uses:
Bacterial infections.

Dosage:
250mg to 500mg every 8 hours.

Side Effects:
Diarrhea, nausea, allergic reactions.
